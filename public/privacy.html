How It Works

Three simple steps to private payments.

1. Deposit USDC

Choose an amount (10, 100, or 1,000 USDC). Approve the spend, then deposit. You'll receive a secret note — save it carefully, it's your only way to withdraw.

2. Share or Hold

Keep your note for later withdrawal, or share it with someone as a private payment. The note is the key — whoever has it can withdraw the USDC.

3. Withdraw Privately

Paste your note and enter any recipient address. The contract verifies your secret + nullifier on-chain and sends USDC to the recipient.

Under the Hood

Built on proven cryptography.

Commitment-Based Verification

Deposits create a cryptographic commitment from a secret + nullifier. Only someone who knows both values can withdraw — verified directly on-chain.

Nullifier Protection

Each note can only be spent once. Nullifier hashes prevent double-spending without revealing your identity or which deposit was used.

Agent Integration

Use the privacy pool to shield and unshield USDC directly on Base for anonymous transactions.
